Mkuranga, September 2024

The pilot ran with twelve young artists, closing in a public exhibition on 14 September.

Every cohort so far has been paid for personally by our director, Ibrahim Alibariki Jonathan, out of the design practice. No grant, no sponsor, no external funding — just a conviction that these children deserve to see their own ideas on a screen.
