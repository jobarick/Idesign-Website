Badili Bongo

Sustainable Transformation & Animation Production

Children's stories, animated.

Young artists write, draw and voice their own stories. We animate them, screen them in their own community, and publish every film on YouTube.

How the children make a film

01 Gather Games, trust, and the stories they already carry.
02 Draw Each child illustrates their own story.
03 Voice They record the narration themselves.
04 Animate Our studio builds the 3D film around their work.
05 Screen A public exhibition, in their own community.

Alumni carry on into paid internships with the studio.
