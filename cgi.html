How we work

Many studios only design products, or only make adverts. We do both in one team, so you get finished work without managing several studios.

Based in Dar es Salaam, we work with brands and manufacturers in Nairobi, Dubai, China, Europe and beyond. You can pay per project, by monthly agreement, or for the right to use a design.

We design football kits for Tanzanian clubs, Simba and Yanga among them. We also make sample models of new products, so you can see and test a design before paying for factory production.
