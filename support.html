What Badili Bongo is

Badili Bongo runs short programmes where children write their own stories, draw them, and record their own narration. Our studio animates each story in 3D around the children's own work, then screens the finished film publicly in their own community before publishing it on YouTube.

Alumni carry on into paid internships with the studio.

Why it needs support

Every cohort run so far has been funded personally by our director, Ibrahim Alibariki Jonathan, together with the design practice. As the programme grows to reach more districts, that no longer covers it on its own. A contribution here goes directly toward workshop materials, the animation production itself, and the community screening that closes each cohort.

Mkuranga, September 2024

The pilot ran with twelve young artists, closing in a public exhibition on 14 September. Six of the finished films are public now.
