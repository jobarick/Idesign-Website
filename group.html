What we believe

All five share one idea: our work is shaped by where it is made. Dar es Salaam's materials, light and daily life inspire our buildings, products, shop and software, made to a standard that works anywhere in the world.

How they work together

Each part has its own clients, and most never need another. If you need more than one service (for example a building, then furniture, then curtains), we pass you between our own teams, and one team stays responsible for the whole job.

You can choose this, but you never have to.
